<objective>
Refactor the elitism path to defer the elite clone (D-10), optionally eliminate the discretionary local-search clone, and prove the phase-success criteria with a before/after `rastrigin` benchmark.

`extract_elite()` currently clones the top-k chromosomes immediately (`generation.rs:427`). D-10 makes the extract phase allocation-free by returning `Vec<usize>` indices and deferring the clone.

CRITICAL CORRECTNESS CONSTRAINT (read carefully): in the generation loop, `extract_elite` runs at `mod.rs:~1729` (right after offspring are merged into the population) and `reinsert_elite` runs at `mod.rs:~1774` — AFTER survivor selection has reordered and truncated the population. Indices computed before survivor selection are STALE afterward. Therefore the elite chromosomes MUST be cloned from the SAME population state the indices were computed against (the pre-survivor-selection population), NOT from the reordered population. The clone is therefore performed at the extract call site (immediately after `extract_elite` returns the indices, while the population still matches those indices), producing an owned `Vec<U>` that is then passed unchanged into `reinsert_elite` after survivor selection. This keeps the same total clone count as today while making `extract_elite` itself allocation-free, and avoids the silent corruption that a literal "clone at reinsertion time against the reordered population" reading of D-10 would cause.

Purpose: Makes the extract phase allocation-free (no intermediate `Vec<U>` inside the function), keeps the same final clone count, and is the place we measure the cumulative win from Plans 01-03.
Output: index-based `extract_elite`, snapshot-clone flow in the loop, optional discretionary clone removal, and `75-BENCH-RESULTS.md`.
</objective>
