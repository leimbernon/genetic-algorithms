<objective>
Refactor NSGA-II, NSGA-III, and MOEA/D engines to: (1) remove the `objective_fns: Vec<Arc<ObjectiveFn<U::Gene>>>` struct field, (2) remove the `with_objective_fns(...)` builder, (3) add a `VectorFitness` bound on `U`, (4) replace closure-driven objective evaluation in `initialize_population()` and offspring evaluation with `chrom.fitness_values().to_vec()`, and (5) add a runtime objective-count check at the start of `run()`. Per D-06, D-07, D-08 and RESEARCH.md Pitfalls 4 + 5. v3.0.0 breaking change.

Purpose: Co-locate objective evaluation with the chromosome via the `VectorFitness` trait — removes Arc closure overhead and external indirection.
Output: Three MO engine files migrated to the new contract. `with_objective_fns` no longer exists on these three engines.
</objective>
