<objective>
Migrate all built-in chromosome implementors and the operator layer to the split trait world. Built-in types (`Binary`, `Range<T>`, `List`) get two impl blocks (ChromosomeT + LinearChromosome). Operators that touch DNA upgrade their bound from `U: ChromosomeT` to `U: LinearChromosome`; operators that touch only fitness/age STAY at `U: ChromosomeT`. The `ValueMutable` supertrait upgrades to `ValueMutable: LinearChromosome`.

Purpose: Completes ARCH-02 across the operator layer. After this plan, all selection/crossover/mutation/survivor/extension operators correctly express their type-level dependency on flat-slice vs. fitness-only chromosomes. Trees and variable-length types (Phases 52, 53) can now opt out of `LinearChromosome` without losing access to fitness-only operators.

Output: 21 operator files with bound-corrected generics, 3 implementor files with two-impl-block splits, ValueMutable trait upgraded, and `tests/structures.rs` test fixture split. Wave 0 tests from 47-01 now pass.
</objective>
