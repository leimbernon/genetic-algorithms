**Objective functions:**
- f1: `dna[0].value` (first variable)

**Builder chain:** `Nsga2Ga::<RangeChromosome<f64>>::new(nsga2_config, ga_config).with_alleles(alleles).with_initialization_fn(move |n, _, _| range_random_initialization(n, Some(&alleles_clone), Some(true))).with_objective_fns(vec![Box::new(obj_f1), Box::new(obj_f2)]).build().expect("Failed to build NSGA-II")`

- Print each point: `"  f1={:.4}, f2={:.4}"` from `individuals[i].objectives[0]` and `[1]`
